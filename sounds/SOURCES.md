## Notes

- `tokyo-night.ogg`: no literal "Tokyo" recording with a CC0 license was found;
  used a substitute per the task brief — a light rain / quiet night city
  street ambience recording, which matches the intended vibe.
- `highway.ogg`: a recording of vehicle traffic passing through a small town
  (continuous road/engine noise, no horns/sirens) was chosen over shorter
  (10-47s) literal "highway"-tagged CC0 clips because it loops far less
  noticeably at 4:15.
- All files were downloaded from Freesound's HQ preview MP3 CDN
  (`cdn.freesound.org/previews/...`), then transcoded to Ogg Vorbis
  (`libvorbis -q:a 4`, 44.1kHz stereo) with ffmpeg for the plugin.
- Original license text observed on every source page: "Creative Commons 0"
  (CC0 1.0 Universal / Public Domain Dedication).
- `birdsong.ogg`: sourced from a recording described as "sunrise birdsong"
  captured at 4:30 AM; chosen because it reads as clearly daytime/morning
  birdsong (no owls, crickets, or other nocturnal ambience) to complement
  the existing `forest-night` category rather than duplicate it.
- `airport.ogg`: a recording of general terminal ambience (crowd murmur,
  distant PA chatter, footsteps) was chosen over clips whose titles flagged
  clear, repeating spoken announcements, since those would stand out badly
  once the file loops.
- `city-street.ogg`: a longer footsteps-and-crowd street recording was
  chosen over a shorter literally-titled "daytime city street" clip (0:31)
  for the same reason `highway.ogg` favored duration over an exact title
  match — it loops far less noticeably.
- `windy-day.ogg`: recorded without a windscreen, so a 90 Hz high-pass was
  applied to tame low-frequency mic rumble.
- `arcade.ogg`: a continuous drone of many machines and players was chosen
  over shorter clips with distinct jingles, which would stand out on loop.
- `cat-cafe.ogg`: no CC0 cat-café recording exists on Freesound (the one cat
  ambience hit carried a no-redistribution note), so this is a mix of the
  bundled `coffee-shop` and `cat-purr` loops — café murmur with a purring cat
  4 dB under it, cut to the coffee-shop length so both seams line up.
- `bowling-alley.ogg`: mono source (upmixed to stereo); trimmed from 14:27 to
  the first 9:00 to keep the file in line with the rest of the catalog.
- `campsite-night.ogg`: a very quiet recording (-56 LUFS), so it needed about
  +30 dB to reach the catalog level; expect a little more hiss than most.
- Loudness: every loop is normalized to about -26 LUFS (two-pass ffmpeg
  `loudnorm`, linear mode, -2 dBTP ceiling) so switching soundscapes doesn't
  need a volume change. The originals ranged from -14 to -54 LUFS.
  `fireplace`, `rain`, `ocean-waves`, `train` and `thunderstorm` were already
  within ~1 LU and were left untouched to avoid another lossy re-encode.
